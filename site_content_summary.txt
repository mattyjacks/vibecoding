[span] Our honey is Kosher, Halal, organic, non-GMO, HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) safe, and passes all 4 major honey quality tests: The C3C4 sugar test, the pollen test, HRMS (high-resolution mass spectrometry), and NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy). All those science words mean there’s no added sugar, the honey comes from the exact regions we say it’s coming from, and there are no toxins like lead or mercury in the honey.
[p] It’s non-crystalizing honey, which means that it stays okay to consume forever. We offer both True Source Certified® and conventional (blended together) varieties.
