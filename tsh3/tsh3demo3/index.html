Laboratory Verification Protocol

🔬 Passes All 4 Major Honey Tests

Every harvest lot undergoes multi-tier laboratory screening before release:

1

C3 / C4 Sugar Analysis

Guarantees zero added high-fructose corn syrup, cane sugar, beet invert sugar, or artificial sweeteners.

2

Microscopic Pollen Test

Verifies botanical and geographical origin, authenticating legitimate multi-floral pollen spectrums.

3

HRMS (High-Res Mass Spectrometry)

Screens for any trace residues, agricultural pesticides, antibiotics, or synthetic impurities down to parts per billion.

4

NMR (Nuclear Magnetic Resonance)

Fingerprints molecular structure to verify natural composition and zero heavy metals like lead or mercury.
