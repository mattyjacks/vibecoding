Laboratory Verification

🔬 Passes All 4 Major Honey Tests

Every batch undergoes strict multi-tier laboratory screening before release:

1

C3 / C4 Sugar Analysis

Guarantees zero added high-fructose corn syrup, cane sugar, beet invert sugar, or artificial sweeteners.

2

Microscopic Pollen Test

Verifies exact botanical and geographical origin, validating authentic multi-floral pollen spectrums.

3

HRMS (High-Resolution Mass Spectrometry)

Screens for any trace residues, pesticides, antibiotics, or synthetic impurities down to parts per billion.

4

NMR (Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy)

Fingerprints the honey's molecular structure, confirming 100% natural composition and zero heavy metals like lead or mercury.
